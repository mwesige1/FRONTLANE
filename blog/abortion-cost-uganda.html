How Much Does Abortion Cost in Kampala, Uganda?

📅 Updated: September 10, 2026 ⏱ 6 min read
👨‍⚕️ Reviewed by Our medical Team

If you're searching for abortion prices in Uganda, you deserve a clear, honest answer — not vague estimates or "contact us for pricing." This article breaks down the real costs of abortion at Frontlane Medical Center in Kampala, what's included, and how pricing reflects the medical complexity of each procedure.

Medical Disclaimer: The information below is for educational purposes and reflects current pricing at Frontlane Medical Center as of September 2026. Abortion in Uganda is legally permitted only in specific circumstances — to save the mother's life, preserve physical or mental health, or in cases of rape, incest, or fetal anomaly. All services at Frontlane are provided within Uganda's legal framework.

What Is Included in the Price?

Unlike many providers, we don't charge separate fees for each component of your care. Every price above includes:

  • Initial consultation with a licensed gynecologist
  • Ultrasound scan and pregnancy dating to confirm gestational age
  • The procedure itself (medical or surgical)
  • All medications — pain relief, bleeding control, infection prevention
  • Post-procedure follow-up visit to confirm recovery
  • Complete aftercare instructions in writing
  • 24/7 access to our emergency support line for any concerns during recovery

There are no hidden fees. The price you are quoted is the price you pay.

We accept two payment methods:

Payment is made at the clinic at the time of your appointment. We do not currently accept card payments, bank transfers, or insurance.

Why Prices Vary by Gestational Age

Abortion pricing in Uganda reflects the medical complexity of the procedure, not arbitrary pricing tiers. Here's how that works:

1–9 weeks: Medical abortion (pills)

At this stage, the pregnancy can be safely terminated using medication. This is the least invasive option. It requires fewer clinical resources, so it costs less. The success rate is 95–98% when properly administered under medical supervision.

10–12 weeks: Surgical abortion

Beyond 9 weeks, medication alone becomes less reliable. A surgical procedure is required, performed in a sterile environment with anesthesia and specialist care. This involves more medical staff, equipment, and monitoring — which increases the cost.

13–25 weeks: Later surgical procedures

Later-term procedures require advanced medical intervention, extended monitoring, and psychological support. Our senior gynecologist leads these procedures personally. The cost reflects the increased complexity and the additional safety protocols required.

Why Cheap Abortion in Uganda Is Dangerous

Some clinics and informal providers in Kampala advertise abortion prices well below market rate. We strongly advise against these options, for one simple reason: safety requires proper medical resources, and those resources cost money.

Low-cost providers typically cut corners on:

  • Sterile facilities — increasing infection risk
  • Qualified medical staff — procedures performed by untrained personnel
  • Pre-procedure assessment — no ultrasound, no dating, no medical history review
  • Post-procedure care — no follow-up, no emergency support
  • Safe medications — counterfeit or expired drugs

The World Health Organization reports that unsafe abortion is a leading cause of maternal death and injury in sub-Saharan Africa. The money saved on a cheap procedure is not worth the risk to your health, fertility, or life.

At Frontlane Medical Center, every procedure is performed by licensed doctors in a sterile environment, with full medical oversight. That's what your payment covers.

No. Our prices reflect the actual cost of safe, medically supervised care. We don't inflate prices to allow for negotiation — what we quote is what we charge.
