Abortion Myths and Facts

📅 Updated: 2 October 2026⏱ 3 min read
👨‍⚕️ Reviewed by Our medical Team

Rumours and stigma spread easily and can push women toward unsafe choices. Here are common myths, with the facts.

Medical Disclaimer: This article is for education and does not replace advice from a doctor. Read our medical disclaimer.

Myth: abortion makes you infertile

Fact: a safe abortion by a trained provider does not generally affect future fertility. Infections from unsafe methods can.

Myth: abortion causes breast cancer

Fact: major reviews have found no link between abortion and breast cancer.

Myth: pills from any pharmacy work the same

Fact: results depend on a genuine medicine, the right regimen, the stage of pregnancy and a health check. See the risks of pharmacy pills.

Myth: you cannot get pregnant right after an abortion

Fact: you can. Fertility returns within about two weeks. See pregnancy after abortion.

Myth: herbs and home methods are a safe alternative

Fact: they are unsafe and can cause serious harm, including bleeding, infection and poisoning.

Myth: only a certain kind of woman has an abortion

Fact: women of every age and background do. Stigma makes it harder to get safe care.

Myth: a clinic can guarantee 100% safety

Fact: no medical procedure is risk-free. Safe care, done well, greatly lowers the risk, and a good clinic is honest about it.

Frequently asked questions
