Abortion Recovery Timeline: Day by Day

📅 Updated: 2 October 2026⏱ 3 min read
👨‍⚕️ Reviewed by Our medical Team

Most women recover quickly, but it helps to know what is normal. This is a general guide. Your doctor will give you instructions for your own situation.

Medical Disclaimer: This article is for education and does not replace advice from a doctor. Read our medical disclaimer.

First 24 hours

Cramping, bleeding and tiredness are common. Rest, drink fluids, eat lightly and use pads, not tampons.

Days 2 to 3

Cramps ease and bleeding becomes lighter for most women. You can return to light activity when you feel ready.

First week

Most women are back to normal activities. Mood can go up and down. Keep your follow-up appointment.

Weeks 2 to 4

Light bleeding or spotting can continue for up to about two weeks. Start contraception if you do not want to become pregnant, as fertility returns quickly.

When your period returns

Usually about four to six weeks after the abortion.

What to avoid

Avoid putting anything in the vagina (tampons, douching, sex) until your doctor says it is safe, often around two weeks. Avoid heavy lifting for the first few days. Get urgent help for heavy bleeding, severe pain, fever or foul-smelling discharge; see post-abortion care.

Frequently asked questions

When can I go back to work?

Many women return within a day or two for light work. Listen to your body and follow your doctor's advice.

When can I have sex again?

Wait until your doctor says it is safe, often around two weeks, and use contraception if you do not want to become pregnant.

Can I exercise?

Start with gentle activity when you feel ready and avoid heavy exercise for the first few days.
