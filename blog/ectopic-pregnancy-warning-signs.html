Ectopic Pregnancy: Warning Signs and Why a Scan Matters

📅 Updated: 2 October 2026⏱ 3 min read
👨‍⚕️ Reviewed by Our medical Team

An ectopic pregnancy grows outside the womb, most often in a fallopian tube. It cannot continue and can be life-threatening, which is why early detection matters.

Medical Disclaimer: This article is for education and does not replace advice from a doctor. Read our medical disclaimer.

Warning signs

  • Pain low on one side of the abdomen or pelvis
  • Vaginal bleeding or spotting that is not like your usual period
  • Pain at the tip of the shoulder
  • Dizziness, fainting, weakness or a fast heartbeat
  • Pain when passing urine or stool

Who is at higher risk

Risk is higher after a previous ectopic pregnancy, tubal surgery or pelvic infection, or if you become pregnant with an IUD in place. It can also happen to women with no risk factors.

Why a scan matters before abortion pills

Abortion pills do not treat an ectopic pregnancy, and bleeding afterwards may be mistaken for a normal abortion while the danger continues. An ultrasound and a health check help find the problem early. See pregnancy test and ultrasound.

What to do

If you have severe one-sided pain, shoulder pain, fainting or heavy bleeding, this is an emergency. Go to the nearest hospital immediately and call +256 759 983 865. See emergency care.

Frequently asked questions

Can abortion pills cause an ectopic pregnancy?

No. Pills do not cause it, but they do not treat it either, which is why a scan first is important.

Can an early scan always see an ectopic pregnancy?

Not always. Very early on it may not be visible, so a repeat scan or tests may be needed if symptoms continue.

Can an ectopic pregnancy be treated?

Yes, with medicine or surgery. Finding it early makes treatment safer.
