Your First Visit to an Abortion Clinic: What to Expect

📅 Updated: 2 October 2026⏱ 3 min read
👨‍⚕️ Reviewed by Our medical Team

Not knowing what happens is often the hardest part. Your first visit is a private conversation and an assessment. You do not have to decide anything on the day.

Medical Disclaimer: This article is for education and does not replace advice from a doctor. Read our medical disclaimer.

Before you arrive

  • Call or WhatsApp +256 759 983 865 and tell us a time that suits you
  • Bring any scan results or medical records you have, and your payment
  • Wear comfortable, loose clothing
  • Bring a friend or family member if you want, or come alone

At the clinic

  • A private welcome and conversation with a doctor or counsellor
  • A pregnancy test if needed and an ultrasound to see how far along you are
  • A short health check and questions about your history
  • A clear explanation of your options, the risks and the price

Your decision

You can take time. You can leave and come back, or choose not to proceed. Nobody will pressure you. If you want to talk it through first, see counselling.

If you go ahead

The doctor explains the plan, asks for your consent and tells you what to expect. You leave with written aftercare instructions, a follow-up date and a number to call at any hour.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the first visit take?

It depends on your situation. Allow time to talk, have a scan and ask questions without rushing.

Do I need to decide on the same day?

No. You can take time to decide, and you can return when you are ready.

Will anyone know I came?

Your records and consultations are private and are not shared without your consent.
