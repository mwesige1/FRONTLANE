How to Choose a Safe Abortion Clinic in Kampala

📅 Updated: 2 October 2026⏱ 3 min read
👨‍⚕️ Reviewed by Our medical Team

Where you go matters more than almost anything else. A good clinic assesses you properly, explains your options and stays available after your visit.

Medical Disclaimer: This article is for education and does not replace advice from a doctor. Read our medical disclaimer.

What a good clinic does

  • A doctor speaks with you privately and listens
  • Confirms the pregnancy and how many weeks you are, usually by ultrasound
  • Explains every option, the risks and the price before you decide
  • Gives written aftercare instructions and a follow-up visit
  • Gives you a number you can call day or night
  • Keeps your records confidential

Ten questions to ask before you book

  • Who will treat me, and are they a qualified doctor?
  • Will I have an ultrasound first?
  • Which methods do you offer for my stage of pregnancy?
  • What exactly does the price include?
  • What pain relief is provided?
  • What happens if there is a complication?
  • Is there a follow-up visit?
  • Can I reach someone after hours?
  • How is my privacy protected?
  • Can I take time to decide?

Red flags

  • Refuses to answer your questions
  • No examination or scan
  • Hands over pills with no guidance
  • Guarantees a risk-free or 100% outcome. No medical procedure is risk-free; safe care lowers the risk
  • No address, no named doctor, or a price that changes

Visit or call before you decide

You are allowed to compare. Call, ask the questions above and read recent Google reviews. At Frontlane you can call or WhatsApp +256 759 983 865 at any hour, or see our pregnancy test and ultrasound service first without committing to anything.

Frequently asked questions

Is a private clinic safer than a pharmacy?

A clinic that examines you, scans you, treats you with a recommended method and follows you up is much safer than buying pills over a counter with no check.

Should I choose the cheapest option?

Compare what is included, not just the price. A low price with no scan, no pain relief and no follow-up can cost far more if something goes wrong.

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes. You can bring a trusted friend or family member, or come alone.
