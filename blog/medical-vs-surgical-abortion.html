Medical vs Surgical Abortion: Which Is Right for You?

📅 Updated: 2 October 2026⏱ 3 min read
👨‍⚕️ Reviewed by Our medical Team

There are two main ways to end a pregnancy: abortion pills (medical abortion) and a short procedure (surgical abortion). Both are safe when provided properly. The right one depends on how far along you are, your health and your preference.

Medical Disclaimer: This article is for education and does not replace advice from a doctor. Read our medical disclaimer.

How to decide

  • How many weeks you are, confirmed by ultrasound
  • Your health history and any medicines you take
  • Whether you want to be at home or at the clinic
  • Privacy and who can support you afterwards
  • How easily you can reach the clinic if you need help

Cost

Medical abortion packages at Frontlane cost UGX 80,000 to UGX 150,000. Surgical abortion costs UGX 200,000 to UGX 800,000 depending on how far along the pregnancy is. See the cost guide.

Frequently asked questions

Which is more effective?

Both are very effective. Pills occasionally need a follow-up medicine or procedure if the abortion is incomplete.

Which is less painful?

It varies. Pills cause cramping over hours; a surgical procedure uses pain management and is short. Your doctor explains what to expect.

Can I switch methods?

If pills do not work, a procedure can complete the abortion. Your doctor will guide you.
