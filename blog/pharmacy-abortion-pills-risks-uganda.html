Buying Abortion Pills at a Pharmacy in Uganda: The Risks

📅 Updated: 2 October 2026⏱ 3 min read
👨‍⚕️ Reviewed by Our medical Team

Many women look for abortion pills at a pharmacy because it seems quick, cheaper and private. The risk is not the pills themselves but taking them without a proper check.

Medical Disclaimer: This article is for education and does not replace advice from a doctor. Read our medical disclaimer.

What can go wrong

  • The medicine may be fake, expired or the wrong one
  • You may be further along than you think, which raises the risk of heavy bleeding
  • An ectopic pregnancy, which pills do not treat, can go unnoticed and become life-threatening
  • Taking pills incorrectly or alone can make them less effective and cause an incomplete abortion
  • Medical conditions, an IUD in place or allergies may make pills unsafe
  • Nobody is there to help if bleeding or pain becomes severe

Why a check first matters

A short consultation lets a doctor date the pregnancy with an ultrasound, rule out an ectopic pregnancy, check your health and give the right medicines with clear instructions. A follow-up visit then confirms that everything is complete. Read how abortion pills work and what a supervised medical abortion includes.

Comparing prices fairly

A pharmacy price can look lower, but our packages (UGX 80,000 to UGX 150,000) also include antibiotics, pain medication, medicine to help control bleeding, written instructions and follow-up. See the full cost guide.

If you have already taken pills

If you have heavy bleeding, severe pain, fever or foul-smelling discharge, get care immediately. We will see you without judgement, whether or not you took the pills with us. See post-abortion care.

Frequently asked questions

Are pharmacy abortion pills real?

Some are, and some are not. You cannot reliably tell by looking at the packet, which is one reason to get medicines through a clinician.

Can I get a check if I took pills elsewhere?

Yes. We can examine you, scan you if needed and treat any problem, with no judgement.

Is a medical abortion safe at home?

It can be when a doctor has first confirmed the pregnancy, given clear instructions and arranged follow-up and a way to reach help. Your doctor decides what suits you.
