Getting Pregnant After an Abortion: Fertility and Timing

📅 Updated: 2 October 2026⏱ 3 min read
👨‍⚕️ Reviewed by Our medical Team

Many women worry that an abortion will affect their ability to have children. For a safe abortion, it generally does not, and fertility returns quickly.

Medical Disclaimer: This article is for education and does not replace advice from a doctor. Read our medical disclaimer.

How soon can you get pregnant?

Ovulation can return within about two weeks, so you can become pregnant before your first period. If you do not want to, start contraception straight away. See contraception methods in Uganda.

Does abortion affect future fertility?

A safe abortion by a trained provider does not generally affect your ability to have children later. Infections or injuries from unsafe methods can.

If you want another pregnancy

Some guidance suggests waiting about six months before the next pregnancy to lower certain risks. Talk to your doctor about what is right for you, and start antenatal care early.

Preparing for a healthy pregnancy

  • See a doctor for a health check
  • Ask about folic acid
  • Avoid alcohol and smoking
  • Treat any infections

If you think you are pregnant again

Do a test and see a doctor. A scan confirms the pregnancy and how far along you are. See pregnancy test and ultrasound.

Frequently asked questions

Will an abortion make me infertile?

A safe abortion does not generally cause infertility.

Can I get pregnant while I am still bleeding?

Yes. Ovulation can return before bleeding stops, so use contraception if you do not want to conceive.

How soon should I start contraception?

Straight away is best. You can discuss methods at your follow-up visit.
