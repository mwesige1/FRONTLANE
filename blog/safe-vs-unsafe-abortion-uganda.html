Safe vs Unsafe Abortion in Uganda: How to Tell the Difference

📅 Updated: 2 October 2026⏱ 3 min read
👨‍⚕️ Reviewed by Our medical Team

Abortion is very safe when it is done with a recommended method, by a trained provider, with proper follow-up. Most serious harm comes from unsafe abortion, so knowing the difference protects your health.

Medical Disclaimer: This article is for education and does not replace advice from a doctor. Read our medical disclaimer.

What makes an abortion safe

The World Health Organization describes safe abortion as one done with a method it recommends, suited to how many weeks pregnant you are, by someone with the right skills. In practice, a safe provider:

  • Examines you and confirms the pregnancy and how far along you are, usually with an ultrasound
  • Offers a recommended method, either abortion pills or a surgical procedure
  • Gives pain relief, clear instructions and a follow-up visit
  • Gives you a number to call if you have problems

Signs of an unsafe abortion

  • No examination, no scan and no questions about your health
  • Pills sold over a counter with no guidance on timing or warning signs
  • Herbs, drinks, inserted objects or any home method
  • A person with no medical training, or a place with no clean equipment
  • No follow-up, no phone number and no way to get help afterwards
  • Pressure to pay or decide immediately, or a price that keeps changing

Why unsafe abortion is dangerous

Unsafe methods can cause heavy bleeding, infection, injury to the womb and an incomplete abortion. These problems can become life-threatening, but they are treatable when you reach care in time.

If you already feel unwell

Do not wait. Heavy bleeding, severe pain, fever or foul-smelling discharge need care straight away. We will not judge you. See our emergency care page and post-abortion care.

Frequently asked questions

What is the safest way to have an abortion?

Have it with a trained clinician who confirms how far along you are, uses a recommended method, manages your pain and arranges follow-up.

Can unsafe abortion affect future pregnancies?

Infections and injuries from unsafe methods can affect your health and, in some cases, future fertility. Safe abortion by a trained provider does not generally affect future fertility.

How can I check that a clinic is safe?

Ask who will treat you, whether you will have an ultrasound, what the price includes and how to reach help afterwards. Our guide to choosing a clinic lists the questions to ask.
