Signs of Incomplete Abortion and Infection

📅 Updated: 2 October 2026⏱ 3 min read
👨‍⚕️ Reviewed by Our medical Team

Sometimes some pregnancy tissue stays in the womb, or an infection develops. Both are treatable when found early.

Medical Disclaimer: This article is for education and does not replace advice from a doctor. Read our medical disclaimer.

Signs of an incomplete abortion

  • Bleeding that stays heavy or does not taper
  • Cramps that continue or return
  • Passing tissue several days after the abortion
  • Pregnancy symptoms that do not settle

Signs of infection

  • Fever or chills (above 38°C)
  • Foul-smelling vaginal discharge
  • Pain or tenderness in the lower abdomen
  • Feeling very unwell

How it is treated

A doctor examines you, may do an ultrasound and treats the cause. Treatment can include antibiotics, medicine to empty the womb or a short procedure. See post-abortion care.

Lowering your risk

Take antibiotics if they were prescribed, keep your follow-up visit and put nothing in the vagina until your doctor says it is safe. If you have any warning signs, call +256 759 983 865 immediately.

Frequently asked questions

How soon after an abortion can infection appear?

It can appear within days. Any fever, foul-smelling discharge or worsening pain needs prompt care.

Can a home pregnancy test confirm the abortion worked?

Not reliably, because a test can stay positive for weeks. A follow-up visit with your doctor is the way to confirm.

Is an incomplete abortion dangerous?

It can cause heavy bleeding or infection if untreated, which is why follow-up matters.
