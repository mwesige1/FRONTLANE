Unplanned Pregnancy in Uganda: Your Options and Where to Get Support

📅 Updated: 2 October 2026⏱ 3 min read
👨‍⚕️ Reviewed by Our medical Team

An unplanned pregnancy can bring fear, confusion and pressure all at once. You are not alone, and you do not have to decide in a hurry.

Medical Disclaimer: This article is for education and does not replace advice from a doctor. Read our medical disclaimer.

First steps

  • Confirm the pregnancy with a test
  • Find out how many weeks you are with an ultrasound
  • Talk to someone you trust, or to a counsellor
  • Give yourself time to think

Thinking through your options

People in this situation consider continuing the pregnancy and parenting, other care arrangements for the child, or ending the pregnancy. Abortion law in Uganda is complicated, so read our guide to the law and speak to a qualified lawyer if you need legal advice.

Questions to ask yourself

  • How do I feel, and what do I want for my future?
  • What support do I have?
  • What does my health allow?
  • Am I making this decision freely, or am I being pressured?

If you are being pressured or feel unsafe

Tell someone you trust. You are entitled to make your own decision, and a counsellor can help you think it through. If you continue the pregnancy, begin antenatal care early.

Private support in Kampala

We offer counselling, a pregnancy test and ultrasound and family planning, without pressure.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know how far along I am?

An ultrasound is the most reliable way. A doctor can date the pregnancy and explain what it means for your options.

Can I talk to someone without deciding anything?

Yes. Counselling is for talking things through, and you do not have to decide.

Is counselling confidential?

Yes. What you share is kept private and is not shared without your consent.
