How Much Does an Abortion Cost in Uganda?

How much is an abortion at a private hospital in Uganda? At Frontlane, abortion pills cost UGX 80,000 to UGX 150,000 and surgical abortion costs UGX 200,000 to UGX 800,000, depending on the package or how far along the pregnancy is. You are told the price before any procedure, and you can pay by cash or mobile money.

Abortion pills (medical abortion)

UGX 80,000

Standard abortion pills for early pregnancy.

UGX 100,000

Two-medicine pill protocol with stronger pain management.

UGX 150,000

Mifepristone and misoprostol, pharmaceutical-grade, with the most comprehensive recovery support.

Every package includes the abortion pills, antibiotics to prevent infection, pain medication for cramping, medicine to help control bleeding, recovery supplements, step-by-step written instructions, ongoing support and follow-up care. Your doctor confirms the right package after a consultation.

Surgical abortion

FromUGX 200,000up toUGX 800,000

The price depends on how far along your pregnancy is. Early pregnancy starts at UGX 200,000. Later pregnancy costs more because the procedure is more involved and needs more monitoring and care. Your doctor confirms your exact price after your ultrasound and before anything is done.
