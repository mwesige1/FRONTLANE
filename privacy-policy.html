Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Frontlane Medical Center respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Details you send us: name, phone, email and your message.
  • Calls and WhatsApp messages you send to us.
  • Basic website usage data (pages visited, device, approximate area) through Google Analytics and Google Ads.

How we use it

To reply to your request, arrange care, improve the website and measure how people find us. We do not sell personal information.

Who processes it

Form submissions pass through formsubmit.co to our email. Google processes analytics and advertising data. A third-party widget displays Google reviews. These providers have their own privacy policies.

Please avoid sensitive details online

Do not put detailed medical information in the website form. Discuss it privately by phone or in person.
