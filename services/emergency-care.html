Emergency Care After an Abortion or Miscarriage in Kampala

Medically reviewed by Dr. Oyesigamukama Nicholus · Last reviewed 2 October 2026

Go now if you have: bleeding that soaks more than two pads in an hour, severe or worsening abdominal pain, fever above 38°C, foul-smelling discharge, fainting or dizziness, or sharp one-sided pain or shoulder pain. Call +256 759 983 865 and go to the nearest hospital.

What to do right now

  1. Call or WhatsApp us on +256 759 983 865. We are open 24 hours. Tell us your symptoms and how long you have had them.
  2. If you feel faint or very unwell, go to the nearest hospital immediately. Do not wait and do not travel alone or drive yourself.
  3. Bring any pill packets, scan results or notes, and someone to support you.
