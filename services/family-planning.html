Family Planning and Contraception in Kampala

Medically reviewed by Our Medical Team · Last reviewed 2 October 2026

Choosing a contraceptive method that fits your body and life helps you plan if and when to have children. We offer private, judgement-free family planning advice in Kampala, including after an abortion or miscarriage.

Methods we can discuss

  • Contraceptive pills taken daily.
  • Injections given every few months.
  • Implants that work for several years.
  • IUD (coil), copper or hormonal, for long-term protection.
  • Condoms, which also protect against infections.
  • Emergency contraception after unprotected sex.

After an abortion

Fertility returns very quickly after an abortion, sometimes within two weeks, so you can become pregnant again before your next period. Starting contraception straight away helps prevent another unplanned pregnancy. Read our family planning guide for Uganda.

Choosing a method

Your doctor considers your health, how long you want protection, side effects and your preferences. You can change your method at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Which family planning method is best?

There is no single best method. The right one depends on your health, how long you want protection and what suits you. A short consultation helps you choose.

Can I get pregnant straight after an abortion?

Yes. Fertility can return within about two weeks, so it is wise to start contraception right away.

What is emergency contraception?

Emergency contraception can reduce the chance of pregnancy after unprotected sex, and works best the sooner it is used. A clinician can advise on the options.
