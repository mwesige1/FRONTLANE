Late Abortion and Later-Term Pregnancy Care in Kampala

Medically reviewed by Dr. Oyesigamukama Nicholus · Last reviewed 2 October 2026

Some women seek care later in pregnancy, for example after a late diagnosis, a change in health or circumstances, or because they could not reach a clinic earlier. Care at this stage needs a more careful assessment, and not every option is available to every woman.

What we do first

Everything begins with a private consultation and an ultrasound. The doctor confirms how far along the pregnancy is, checks your health and explains which options, if any, are safe and possible for you. You are never pressured to decide.

Safety

The risks of an abortion rise as pregnancy advances, so later-term care should only be provided by trained doctors with proper facilities and follow-up. Be cautious of anyone offering pills or a procedure without a scan and assessment.

Support

We offer counselling before and after, plus post-abortion care and family planning advice. Read our guide to late abortion in Uganda and to the law on abortion in Uganda.

Frequently asked questions

Can I have a late abortion in Kampala?

It depends on your assessment and on the law. The first step is a private consultation and ultrasound, after which the doctor explains what is possible and safe for you.

Is a late abortion more risky?

Risks increase as pregnancy advances, which is why a thorough assessment, trained staff and follow-up care are essential.
