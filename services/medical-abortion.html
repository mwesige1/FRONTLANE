Medical Abortion (Abortion Pills) in Kampala, Uganda

Reviewed by Our medical team · Last reviewed 2 October 2026

Medical abortion ends an early pregnancy using medicines instead of surgery. At Frontlane Medical Center in Kampala, abortion pills are given after a doctor has confirmed your pregnancy and how far along you are.

How medical abortion works

Medical abortion uses two medicines, mifepristone and misoprostol, taken in a set order. Mifepristone blocks the hormone that sustains a pregnancy, and misoprostol causes the womb to empty. This combination is the method recommended by the World Health Organization. Your doctor explains exactly how and when to take each medicine.

Is it right for you?

Abortion pills suit many women in early pregnancy, but not everyone. Before giving them, your doctor checks your pregnancy with an ultrasound and asks about your health, because some conditions, such as a pregnancy outside the womb (ectopic pregnancy), make pills unsafe. If pills are not suitable, we discuss other options, including surgical abortion.

What to expect

Expect cramping and bleeding heavier than a normal period, usually within hours of the second medicine. Pain relief is part of every package. Bleeding then eases over days and light bleeding or spotting can continue for up to a couple of weeks. You receive step-by-step written instructions and can call us at any time.

Why not buy abortion pills at a pharmacy?

Pills bought without a check can be the wrong medicine, the wrong stage of pregnancy or unsafe for you, and nobody is there if something goes wrong. A short consultation and ultrasound first makes the process much safer.

Frequently asked questions

How do abortion pills work?

Mifepristone blocks the hormone that sustains the pregnancy and misoprostol causes the womb to empty. Taken together under a doctor's guidance, they end an early pregnancy without surgery.

How long does a medical abortion take?

The pregnancy usually passes within hours of the second medicine, and bleeding then settles over days. Your doctor arranges a follow-up to confirm the abortion is complete.

What are the side effects of abortion pills?

\

Common effects are cramping, heavy bleeding, nausea, diarrhoea and tiredness. Pain relief and written instructions are included, and severe symptoms need urgent care.

How much do abortion pills cost in Uganda?

At Frontlane, medical abortion packages cost UGX 80,000, UGX 100,000 or UGX 150,000, each including medicines for infection, pain and bleeding plus follow-up.
