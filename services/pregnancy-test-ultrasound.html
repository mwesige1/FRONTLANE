Pregnancy Test and Ultrasound Scan in Kampala

Medically reviewed by Dr. Oyesigamukama Nicholus · Last reviewed 2 October 2026

If you think you are pregnant, the first step is to confirm it and find out how far along you are. We offer private pregnancy testing and ultrasound in Kampala.

Pregnancy test

A urine or blood test confirms pregnancy. A home test is usually reliable from around the time of a missed period, and a clinic can confirm the result.

Why an ultrasound matters

  • Dating: shows how many weeks pregnant you are, which decides which options are safe.
  • Location: helps identify a pregnancy outside the womb (ectopic), which needs urgent care.
  • Wellbeing: checks that the pregnancy is developing normally.

An ultrasound should always come before abortion pills or a procedure. This is one reason buying pills without a scan is risky.

What to expect

The visit is private and quick. A clinician explains what the scan shows and your options. You are never obliged to continue with any service.

Frequently asked questions

How early can a pregnancy be detected?

Most tests can detect pregnancy from around the time of a missed period, and an ultrasound can usually see an early pregnancy a little later.

Why do I need an ultrasound before abortion pills?

It confirms how many weeks you are and helps rule out an ectopic pregnancy, so your doctor can choose a safe option.
