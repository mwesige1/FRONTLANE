Surgical Abortion in Kampala, Uganda

Reviewed by Our medical Team · Last reviewed 2 October 2026

Surgical abortion is a short procedure that gently empties the womb. At Frontlane it is carried out by a doctor in a clean clinical setting, with pain management, after a full assessment.

When is surgical abortion recommended?

It may suit you if abortion pills are not suitable, if pills did not work, if the pregnancy is further along, or if you prefer a single-visit procedure. Your doctor decides after an ultrasound and a health check. See also our medical abortion option.

What happens

  1. Consultation: a private talk, pregnancy confirmation and ultrasound to date the pregnancy.
  2. Preparation: health check and a clear explanation of the procedure and pain control.
  3. Procedure: usually completed in a short time, with a clinician monitoring you throughout.
  4. Recovery: rest at the clinic, aftercare instructions and a follow-up visit.

Risks

Serious problems are uncommon when the procedure is done by trained staff, but they can include heavy bleeding, infection, an incomplete abortion or injury to the womb. This is why a proper clinic, with follow-up and 24-hour contact, matters.

Cost

A surgical abortion at Frontlane costs between UGX 200,000 and UGX 600,000 , depending on how far along the pregnancy is. Early pregnancy starts at UGX 200,000. Later pregnancy costs more because the procedure is more involved and needs more monitoring and care.

Stage of pregnancy Price (UGX)
Early pregnancy from 200,000
Later pregnancy up to 600,000

> The cost article lists 10-17 weeks at 200,000-400,000 and 18-25 weeks at 400,000-600,000

Your doctor confirms your exact price after your ultrasound and before any procedure. Payment is by cash or mobile money. See the abortion cost guide .

Frequently asked questions

Is surgical abortion painful?

Pain management is used during the procedure, so most women feel pressure and cramping rather than severe pain. Your doctor explains the options beforehand.

How long does recovery take after a surgical abortion?

Many women feel able to resume light activity within a day or two. Cramping and light bleeding can continue for a few days, and we arrange a follow-up visit.

Which pregnancy stage can have a surgical abortion?

It depends on your ultrasound and health. Your doctor confirms what is safe for you at your consultation.

How much does a surgical abortion cost in Kampala?

A surgical abortion at Frontlane costs between UGX 200,000 and UGX 600,000, starting at UGX 200,000 for early pregnancy and rising with the stage of pregnancy. Your doctor confirms your exact price after your ultrasound and before any procedure.
